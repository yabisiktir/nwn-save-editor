WHY IT HOLDS AT 16
  No two touching golds share a value: every gold meets either the dark
  channel (#14100C) or parchment. The 16px read is pure shape --
  round metal vs wide parchment -- plus a value difference, so the two
  never swap in a crowded dock. Both marks fill the same optical circle
  inside the same plaque and rim, lit from the top.
